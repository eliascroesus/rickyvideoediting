# Asset library: music & sound effects

Everything here is **free to use in videos, including monetized ones**.

| Folder | What | License | Do I need to credit? |
|---|---|---|---|
| [`music/`](music/) | 32 tension / intensity tracks by Kevin MacLeod | CC BY 4.0 | **Yes**: paste the credit line in your caption/description ([CREDITS.md](CREDITS.md)) |
| [`sfx/`](sfx/) | 796 sound effects | CC0 (public domain) | No |

Sound effects are 48 kHz WAV (works in CapCut, Premiere, Resolve, Final Cut, iMovie).
Music is the original 256–320 kbps MP3 from the composer.

## Sound effects: `assets/sfx/`

### `cinematic-generated/`: risers, booms & braams (made for this repo)

Synthesized from scratch with [`tools/synth_cinematic.py`](../tools/synth_cinematic.py), so they're
original and public domain (CC0). This is the "trailer" toolkit that free libraries usually lack.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `riser-noise-3s` / `-6s` | Noise sweep rising in pitch & volume. Ends at its peak, so cut to the next shot there |
| `riser-tonal-4s` / `-8s` / `-12s` | Rising pitch + noise with pulses that speed up (big tension build) |
| `riser-into-boom-5s` | 5 s riser that lands on a boom: drop it in and line up the boom with your cut |
| `reverse-swell-2s` / `-4s` | Reverse-cymbal "sucking in" swell, peaks at the end (put the end on the cut) |
| `boom-cinematic-1` / `-2-deep` / `-3-punchy` | Trailer-style impact with sub + reverb tail |
| `sub-drop-short` / `-long` | Deep falling bass "drop" for big moments |
| `braam-low` / `braam-dark` | Huge low brass-like "BRAAAM" |
| `whoosh-fast` / `-medium` / `-slow` | Air whooshes that pan across the stereo field: transitions |
| `tension-drone-30s` | Dark, dissonant low drone bed (fades in/out) |
| `accelerating-pulse-12s` | Heartbeat-like sub thumps speeding up from 60 → 160 BPM |
