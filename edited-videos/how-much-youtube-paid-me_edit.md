# how-much-youtube-paid-me_edit.mp4 (v2, Iman Gadzhi style)

Edit of `This is how much youtubee paid me for 144m views` (raw: GitHub Release `raw-videos`).
**2560x1440 (native 1440p), 30 fps, high bitrate (x264 CRF 14)**, about 10:01, audio at -14 LUFS.
Rebuild with `tools/precompute_paid_me.py` (person cutouts + paper stabilization), then
`tools/edit_youtube_paid_me.py` (video chunks + audio).

## Style system (from the Iman Gadzhi breakdown)

- **Colour 60/30/10:** near-black navy base, slate grey secondary, warm gold accent.
- **Two fonts:** Inter Display SemiBold for normal words, EB Garamond Bold Italic in gold with a deep glow
  for the special words.
- **Text animation:** per-letter write-on (rises in, blur to sharp, yellow to final colour), posterized to
  15 fps, 3D tilt, soft shadow. On the talking head the words sit on either side of Ricky's head
  ("last month I made" | "$40,000"), with the background blurred and darkened behind him and him kept sharp.
- **UI cards:** rounded rectangles, grey to black gradient, light-sweep edge with a glow, faded line icons
  clipped to the card. They pop in with scale, rise and a 3D tilt settling flat (fast in, slow settle).
- **Camera:** continuous eased moves (it never fully stops), punch-ins on key lines, zoom-through transitions.
- **Paper shots:** stabilized (vertical jitter cut about 5x), graded and followed by the camera.
- **Screen recordings:** framed as a rounded window on a dark grid, with spotlight (dim + blur everything
  except the focus area) on the important parts.
